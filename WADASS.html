Mark Zuckerberg

Mark Zuckerberg is an American technology entrepreneur, philanthropist, and the co-founder, chairman, and CEO of Meta Platforms (formerly Facebook, Inc.). Born on May 14, 1984, in White Plains, New York, he launched Facebook from his Harvard University dorm room in 2004 alongside his roommates. The platform revolutionized global communication, quickly transforming from a college networking site into the world's largest social media network and shaping the modern digital age.As the driving force behind Meta, Zuckerberg has overseen the strategic acquisitions of major platforms like Instagram and WhatsApp, expanding the company's global footprint. In 2021, he rebranded the parent company to Meta to signal a strategic pivot toward building the metaverse—an interconnected virtual reality ecosystem aimed at defining the next generation of social connection and computing.Beyond his business pursuits, Zuckerberg is a prominent philanthropist. Alongside his wife, Priscilla Chan, he founded the Chan Zuckerberg Initiative (CZI) in 2015. The organization pledges 99% of their Facebook shares over their lifetimes to advance human potential and promote equality in areas such as science, education, justice, and economic opportunity. Zuckerberg remains one of the most influential and closely watched figures in technology and global business.

Major Contributions

🌐 Facebook

Co-founded Facebook in 2004, creating a social networking platform that transformed the way people communicate and connect online.

📱 Instagram

Under Meta, Instagram became one of the world's leading platforms for sharing photos, videos, stories and short-form content.

💬 WhatsApp

Meta's acquisition of WhatsApp expanded Zuckerberg's company's presence in global messaging and digital communication.

🏢 Meta Platforms

In 2021, Facebook, Inc. was renamed Meta Platforms, reflecting Zuckerberg's broader vision for social technology and the metaverse.

🥽 Metaverse

Zuckerberg has invested heavily in virtual and augmented reality technologies as part of Meta's long-term vision for immersive digital experiences.

🎓 Education & Philanthropy

Through the Chan Zuckerberg Initiative, Zuckerberg and his wife Priscilla Chan support projects in science, education and technology.
